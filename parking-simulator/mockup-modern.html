Design principles
Warm, considered, operational — not gamey.
A training tool that reads as a talabat product first and a game second. Every visual choice reinforces the operational story: you're a rider on a real job, in a real city, being taught to park with respect.
01

Warm cinema, not neon arcade

Sodium-lamp basement, golden-hour porte-cochère. The world is lit like a film, not a puzzle app.

02

Brand orange used hot

Reserved for CTA, target zones, and success accents — never as a wash. Restraint gives it weight.

03

Type carries the personality

Fraunces display against a clean Inter body. Serif restraint tells the CEO this is a considered product.

04

Motion is honest

250ms open, 150ms close on modals. Cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). No slide-from-edge cheapness anywhere.

Screen flow
Splash → Select → Dispatch → Drive → Result.
Six taps from launch to a scored run. Every screen is thumb-reachable, the timer is visible without covering the road, and the coaching copy on failure treats the rider as a professional, not a player.
