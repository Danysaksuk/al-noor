# Growth & Monetization Playbook

Operating guide for running the channel day-to-day once the pipeline is live.

## Daily (5-10 min of your time)
- Approve/reject today's script + video in Telegram (the compliance-critical
  human-direction step — don't rubber-stamp, actually read it).
- Reply to top comments on the last 24h of posts within the first hour of
  each post going live — early engagement velocity is a ranking signal.
- Post at the same time each day (habitual-viewer effect).

## Weekly (15-20 min)
- Pull performance data: views, completion rate, shares, saves, follower
  delta. Postiz analytics + TikTok's native creator analytics both work.
- Identify the top 1-2 performing videos of the week; note the topic/hook
  pattern and feed it back into `prompts/topic-research.md` as a
  "double down on" signal.
- Drop any topic angle that underperformed twice in a row.

## Core levers, ranked by leverage
1. **Hook (first 2 seconds)** — biggest single lever on completion rate,
   which is now the #1 ranking signal (2026 algorithm: 70%+ completion
   needed to go viral, up from 50% in 2024).
2. **Consistency** — same time, at least daily once the pipeline is stable
   (research floor is 3-5x/week; daily compounds faster).
3. **Length** — 60-180s outperforms 15s clips in 2026 and is required for
   Creator Rewards eligibility (≥60s).
4. **Native voice + captions** — auto-transcripts are indexed for TikTok
   search; scripts should be naturally keyword-rich for the niche.
5. **Niche discipline** — one clear topic per video; don't mix messages.

## Monetization checklist
- [ ] Confirm TikTok account's registered country is on the Creator
      Rewards eligible list (US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, BR core, expanding).
- [ ] Reach 10,000 followers.
- [ ] Reach 100,000 valid views in the trailing 30 days (rolling window —
      doesn't bank from an old viral spike).
- [ ] All videos ≥60 seconds.
- [ ] Account in good standing (no active strikes).
- [ ] Apply to Creator Rewards Program once all above are true.
- [ ] In parallel: apply to niche-relevant affiliate programs (e.g.
      budgeting apps/cards for a finance niche) — this can pay out before
      the platform threshold is hit, and stacks with Creator Rewards later.

## What NOT to do
- Don't switch niches once picked — the algorithm and audience both reward
  consistency; changing topic mid-stream resets momentum.
- Don't use unofficial/ToS-violating upload bots — ban risk outweighs the
  marginal automation gained over Postiz's approved posting path.
- Don't fully rubber-stamp every video without reading it — beyond the
  compliance angle, this is your actual quality control.
- Don't chase every trend outside the niche — off-niche virality doesn't
  build a monetizable, algorithmically-trusted channel.
